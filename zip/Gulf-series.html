About Gulf Series

The Khidkie™ Gulf Series is engineered as a robust, high-performance sliding system for projects where strength, stability and smoothness are non-negotiable. With specially designed deep bottom frames, verticals and intelligent track/top adapters, the Gulf Series handles larger panel sizes, heavier glass and frequent daily usage with confidence.

Where Gulf Series Works Best

Gulf Series is ideal for large balcony sliders, hall elevations, premium apartments, villas, commercial frontages and high-traffic openings. The deeper bottom frames and dedicated adapters keep the system aligned over time, reducing sagging and performance issues in long-span openings. It is particularly suited for coastal, high-wind and high-rise projects where structural strength and long-term reliability are critical.

Why Fabricators Prefer Gulf Series

Fabricators choose the Gulf Series for its intelligent profile ecosystem: dedicated 2T and 3T bottoms, matching verticals, track adapters and top adapters that simplify fabrication and installation. The system is designed to integrate cleanly with standard hardware, rollers and glass thicknesses, while providing a premium, clean and modern aesthetic that appeals to architects and homeowners alike.

KHIDKIE™ 26MM GULF SERIES

Performance-Focused 26mm Sliding System. Strong, Clean & Versatile.

The 26mm Gulf Series is a performance-focused sliding system that balances strength, aesthetics and material efficiency. With a 26mm frame depth, it offers a slightly leaner geometry than the 29mm version, yet retains the core Gulf DNA of strong bottom sections, matching verticals and intelligent adapter profiles for everyday premium use.

The ecosystem is built around 26MM GULF 2T BOTTOM (26010) and 26MM GULF 3T BOTTOM (26012), supported by 26MM GULF 2T VERTICAL (26011) and 26MM GULF 3T VERTICAL (26013). This combination allows fabricators to create 2-track and 3-track sliding windows and doors that feel stable, run smooth and look visually proportionate for residential and mid-sized commercial applications.

The 26MM GULF TRACK CAP (26014) and 26MM GULF TRACK ADAPTER (26015) are engineered to refine the finished look and interface cleanly with surrounding surfaces, ensuring neat terminations, improved protection for tracks and easier cleaning. The 26MM GULF TOP ADAPTER (26019) and 26MM GULF TOPBOTTOM (26021) offer further flexibility for top and combination uses, simplifying stock and fabrication planning.

The result is a highly practical system that delivers:
• Strong and stable performance for everyday openings
• Clean, straight visual lines suitable for modern interiors
• Neat track finishing with dedicated caps & adapters
• Ease of fabrication and installation for fabricators
• Compatibility with modern finishes like matte black and textured coatings

For projects demanding a premium yet efficient aluminium sliding solution, the 26mm Gulf Series provides a refined, future-ready platform that integrates smoothly into the Khidkie™ product universe.

KHIDKIE™ 29MM GULF SERIES

Deep-Frame Heavy-Duty Sliding System. Built for Large Spans & Demanding Projects.

The 29mm Gulf Series from Khidkie™ is a deep, heavy-duty sliding window and door system engineered for high-performance scenarios. With a 29mm depth profile, this system delivers excellent structural rigidity, making it ideal for large sliding windows, balcony doors and hall openings where standard systems struggle to retain alignment, straightness and smoothness over time.

The system is intelligently structured around dedicated 2-track and 3-track bottom profiles, paired with matching verticals and specialised adapters. The 29MM GULF 2T BOTTOM and 29MM GULF 3T BOTTOM are designed to carry higher loads and maintain roller geometry across longer spans, while the 29MM GULF 2T VERTICAL and 29MM GULF 3T VERTICAL provide robust support for tall and wide sashes.

The 29MM GULF TRACK ADAPTER and 29MM GULF TOP ADAPTER enable neat integration with surrounding building elements and other aluminium systems, simplifying fabrication and installation in real site conditions. The 29MM GULF TOPBOTTOM profile is a versatile section that can be used as top or bottom where design demands a consistent aesthetic or simplified stock management.

Together, these profiles create a flexible, high-strength ecosystem that delivers:
• Excellent load distribution over long spans
• Improved straightness and panel alignment
• Smooth rolling with compatible heavy-duty rollers
• Reduced deflection and rattling under wind loads
• A clean, premium appearance with deep, confident lines

If your project demands a strong, reliable, long-term solution for balcony doors, hall openings, large picture sliders or premium façades, the 29mm Gulf Series stands out as a dependable, performance-first choice that still maintains the refined visual language of the Khidkie™ design identity.

KHIDKIE™ 35 × 59 GULF SERIES

Ultra-Heavy Sliding System. Engineered for Wide Spans, Tall Panels & Premium Architecture.

The 35×59 Gulf Series from Khidkie™ is an ultra-robust sliding window and door system designed for projects where scale, strength, and long-term performance are non-negotiable. With a large 35×59mm profile geometry, this series delivers exceptional structural depth and load-bearing capacity, making it ideal for wide balcony sliders, tall glass panels, and expansive openings in premium residential and commercial buildings.

Unlike lighter sliding systems, the 35×59 Gulf Series is purpose-built to handle heavier shutters and increased glass weight without compromising alignment or smooth operation. Dedicated 2-track and 3-track bottom profiles form a solid foundation, while matching vertical sections ensure consistent strength, straightness, and visual balance across full-height installations.

The system includes precision-engineered shutter profiles, slim and slim KPA interlock options, and a dedicated meeting section to ensure tight closure, reduced vibration, and controlled panel movement across wide spans. These elements work together to maintain stability even under continuous use and external wind pressure.

To support complex architectural detailing, the 35×59 Gulf Series is complemented by top-bottom profiles, SGU adapters, track adapters, and U- and V-type track caps. These components simplify on-site integration, allow clean junctions with walls and ceilings, and help fabricators achieve a refined, premium finish without compromising structural integrity.

Together, the 35×59 Gulf ecosystem delivers:
• Exceptional load-handling capability for heavy shutters
• Superior straightness across wide and tall openings
• Stable roller performance over long track lengths
• Reduced deflection, vibration, and panel noise
• A bold, confident visual profile suited to premium façades

For projects that demand maximum strength, long-span reliability, and a commanding architectural presence — from luxury residences to large commercial openings — the 35×59 Gulf Series stands as Khidkie™’s most powerful sliding solution, engineered to perform flawlessly over years of use.
