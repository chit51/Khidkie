Khidkie

Khidkie is the premium fenestration and architectural brand marketed by the Shreenathji corporate group. We operate as a comprehensive fabrication company, dedicated to manufacturing bespoke windows, doors, and a wide array of advanced aluminium architectural solutions.

End-to-End Project Development

We pride ourselves on offering a complete A-to-Z service model. Khidkie does not just manufacture; we handle full-scale project development. From the precision fabrication of all aluminium products in our facility to the final, flawless installation on project development sites, we deliver a turnkey solution for architects, builders, and direct clients.

At a Glance

  • Focus: A-to-Z Fenestration Solutions
  • Services: Fabrication & Installation
  • Market: Architectural Project Development

Shreenathji Aluminium

Shreenathji Aluminium is the flagship business of the group, operating as a massive wholesale distributor and an innovative manufacturing powerhouse for aluminium extrusion products.

Exclusive Dies & Pan-India Supply

We differentiate ourselves through in-house innovation. Our team designs exclusive dies, bringing entirely new and proprietary extrusion series to the market. Supplying over 15 distinct series—including comprehensive sections, profiles, and hardware—our reach extends Pan-India. We serve as the trusted backbone for a massive network of fabricators, retailers, wholesalers, contractors, and corporate partners.

At a Glance

  • Headquarters: Rajkot, Gujarat
  • Capability: Exclusive Die Design
  • Inventory: 15+ Extrusion Series
  • Distribution: Pan-India B2B Network

Shreenathji Metal

Shreenathji Metal is our specialized distribution wing built to service complex, out-of-state geographic markets. This vertical thrives on maintaining robust relationships with a wide network of out-of-state dealers and distributors.

Authorized Jindal Dealer & Diverse Inventory

Operating as a non-exclusive authorized dealer for Jindal Aluminium, Shreenathji Metal provides market flexibility. We offer our dealers a comprehensive and highly competitive inventory that includes both premium Jindal products alongside our expansive non-Jindal series, ensuring our partners have the exact materials required for any scale of project.

At a Glance

  • Headquarters: Rajkot, Gujarat
  • Partnership: Authorized Jindal Dealer
  • Inventory: Jindal & Non-Jindal Series
  • Market Focus: Out-of-State Dealer Networks
