KHIDKIE™ 29MM VENTANA SERIES

A deep-frame, heavy-duty aluminium sliding system engineered for large openings, higher load capacity, and long-term dimensional stability.

The 29MM Ventana Series from Khidkie™ is a structurally driven sliding system developed around dedicated deep-track and frame profiles rather than lightweight or adapted sliding sections. The system begins with purpose-built 29MM 2-Track and 3-Track Frames, designed to provide higher rigidity, improved load distribution, and accurate shutter alignment across wide openings.

Structural load management in the Ventana Series is handled through its deep 29mm bottom track profiles, which are engineered to support heavier shutters while maintaining smooth and stable sliding motion. These bottom tracks work in coordination with matching top profiles and verticals to ensure consistent movement, controlled shutter travel, and long-term straightness under repeated operation.

The system supports multiple shutter configurations based on performance and glazing requirements. The 29MM Standard Shutter and 29MM Square Shutter are used for single-glazed applications where clean aesthetics and balanced weight are required, while the 29MM DGU Shutter is proportioned to accommodate double-glazed units for improved acoustic performance, thermal insulation, and structural stiffness. Each shutter profile is designed to remain rigid under load while enabling smooth linear sliding.

Shutter interaction, sealing, and alignment are controlled using a range of dedicated interlock profiles rather than universal meeting sections. The series includes 29MM Standard Interlock, Slim Interlock, KPA Interlock, and DGU-specific Interlocks, allowing the system to be configured precisely based on shutter weight, glazing type, and performance requirements.

The Ventana Series further supports integrated architectural elements through dedicated profiles such as Grill-Compatible Frames, 3-in-1 Frames, Track Caps, Meeting Patti, H Adapters, and 29MM Middle Profiles. These components ensure clean finishing, structural continuity, and controlled transitions across multi-shutter and multi-track configurations.

Unlike lightweight sliding systems, the Ventana Series is engineered around structural depth and controlled linear motion rather than minimal profile thickness. Every component — frames, shutters, interlocks, and accessories — is designed to function as a unified system, ensuring:
• High load-bearing capacity for large shutters
• Smooth and stable sliding motion over time
• Accurate shutter alignment across wide spans
• Compatibility with grills, mesh systems, and DGU glazing
• Long-term durability in demanding residential and commercial use

With its deep 29mm frame architecture, multiple track options, heavy-duty shutter profiles, and system-matched accessories, the KHIDKIE™ Ventana Series is ideally suited for large sliding windows and doors in balconies, villas, apartments, cafés, showrooms, and light commercial spaces where strength, scale, and smooth sliding performance are functional requirements — not optional features. The Ventana Series is not a lightweight sliding adaptation; it is a purpose-built heavy-duty sliding system designed for precision, stability, and architectural scale.
