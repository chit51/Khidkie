KHIDKIE™ 26MM DOMAL SERIES

The Original European-Style Sliding System. Iconic Strength, Smooth Performance & Timeless Design.

The 26mm Domal Series from Khidkie™ is one of the most complete, versatile, and time-tested aluminium sliding systems ever engineered. Inspired by the precision of traditional Italian Domal architecture and redesigned for Indian climate, performance, and aesthetics — this series represents the true foundation of modern sliding window engineering.

What sets the 26mm Domal Series apart is its unmatched configuration flexibility. With an extensive range of 2-track, 3-track, 4-track, grill-integrated frames, cover frames, plain frames, and multi-track (3-in-1) combinations, this system adapts to every home, every elevation, and every functional requirement effortlessly. It is the system that architects, builders, and homeowners choose when they want balance — balance between strength and elegance, performance and affordability, durability and design.

The engineering is intentionally robust. The bottom frames ensure straightness over long spans, even with years of use. The shutter options, including Euro shutter, standard Domal shutter, 26×60 shutter, SGU and DGU options, provide flexibility for acoustic needs, insulation levels, and shutter weight requirements.

Every interlock, from standard 26mm to slim and slim-rain versions, is crafted to deliver:
• Smoother sliding
• Better sealing
• Reduced rattle
• Improved wind resistance
• Quiet & frictionless performance

The 26mm Domal Series supports advanced glazing, including SGU and DGU shutters, allowing homeowners to upgrade both comfort and energy efficiency. Whether it’s a compact bedroom slider, a balcony opening, or a grand 4-track hall window, the 26mm Domal Series moulds itself to the design — maintaining a classic European profile, clean lines, and an elegance that never goes out of style. This is more than a sliding system. It is the backbone of modern aluminium architecture, and a testament to why Domal remains one of the most respected names in the fenestration world.

KHIDKIE™ 26MM SLIM DOMAL SERIES

Ultra-Slim European Look. Seamless Views.
Minimal frame. Maximum beauty.

The 26mm Slim Domal Series from Khidkie™ is crafted for one purpose — to bring maximum glass visibility and minimum aluminium obstruction into modern Indian homes. This series redefines aesthetics with its ultra-slim, ultra-refined profile geometry inspired by European slimline window architecture.

Where traditional sliding systems feel chunky, the Slim Domal Series introduces a super-lean visual profile, making the aluminium almost disappear and allowing wide, uninterrupted views. Whether it’s a living room opening to a balcony, a bedroom slider, or an elevation window, Slim Domal adds an unmistakable premium character to any space.

The system features streamlined 2-track and 3-track frames, engineered with precision to maintain strength despite their reduced thickness. The slim verticals, refined bottom frames, and minimal interlocks are designed to create that signature “thin-line” appearance architects love — without compromising stability or performance.

Thanks to high-grade aluminium and superior extrusion quality, the Slim Domal Series delivers buttery-smooth sliding, excellent alignment, reduced friction, and long-term straightness. The frames pair seamlessly with SGU and DGU shutter profiles from your 26mm ecosystem, offering flexibility depending on the project’s acoustic or thermal needs.

Beautiful, durable, and tailored for high-end apartments, villas, elevations, offices, and premium interior spaces — the 26mm Slim Domal Series is the system you choose when the goal is to make the home look sleek, modern, and elite, without a single extra millimetre of aluminium blocking the view.

KHIDKIE™ 23MM MINI DOMAL SERIES

Compact European Design. Beautifully Engineered.
Compact profile – Grand performance.

The Mini Domal 23mm Series from Khidkie™ represents the perfect balance between European-inspired aesthetics and smart Indian engineering. Designed as a compact sliding system, this series brings a clean, minimal, and elegant look — ideal for modern apartments, bedrooms, kitchens, galleries, offices, and interior spaces where efficiency meets beauty.

Crafted with a precise 23mm frame depth, the Mini Domal system maintains a slim and refined visual profile while still providing dependable structural strength. Its carefully matched components — 2-track and 3-track frames, grill-integrated frames, sliding shutters, slim interlocks, and premium box pipes — work together to deliver a smooth, effortless sliding experience that feels premium at every touch.

Each profile is extruded using high-grade aluminium alloy, ensuring durability, corrosion resistance, and long service life even in harsh Indian climates. The geometry is engineered to support smooth-running rollers, secure panel alignment, and neat joinery, making this a reliable choice for daily-use spaces. Whether it’s a compact gallery slider or a functional window for a utilitarian room, Mini Domal handles each requirement with quiet efficiency and refined style.

The availability of grill frames, box pipes, and multi-track configurations offers true flexibility in design — enabling clients to integrate safety, ventilation, and aesthetic appeal seamlessly into their spaces.
