Industries We Cater To

At Khidkie™, we provide high-quality aluminium profiles and hardware to a diverse range of industries, enabling them to build modern, durable, and stylish structures. Our products serve:


  • Construction & Real Estate – Residential, commercial, and industrial buildings.

  • Interior Design & Home Solutions – Aluminium systems for doors, windows, partitions, and decorative designs.

  • Fabricators & Retailers – Supplying a wide range of profiles for direct project requirements.

  • Architectural & Facade Solutions – Precision aluminium sections and hardware for all scales of fabrication projects.

  • Solar & Renewable Energy – Frames and structures for solar panels and mounting systems.

  • Industrial & Manufacturing Units – Specialised aluminium profiles for machinery, partitions, enclosures and engineering projects.

  • Hospitality & Infrastructure – Hotels, resorts, offices, and public facilities.
