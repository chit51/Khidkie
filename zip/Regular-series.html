About Regular Series

The Regular Series is the core sliding window and door platform of the Indian aluminium fenestration industry. Designed for everyday residential, commercial, and project-based construction, this system delivers the optimal balance of affordability, durability, and functional performance across millions of installations. With multiple track options and frame configurations, it adapts to nearly every sliding requirement.

Where Regular Series Works Best

The Regular Series is ideally suited for apartments, independent houses, rental properties, offices, shops, schools, hospitals, and builder-grade housing projects. Its flexibility in 2-track, 3-track and 4-track configurations allows it to serve small bedroom windows as well as wide living room and balcony openings with equal efficiency.

Why Fabricators Prefer Regular Series

Fabricators rely on the Regular Series because of its predictable section logic, consistent weights, easy cutting, and fast assembly. With clearly defined top, bottom, handle, interlock and middle profiles in both 3/4" and 1" formats, the system enables high-volume fabrication with low error rates and reliable on-site installation.

KHIDKIE™ REGULAR SERIES SYSTEM

A modular, multi-track aluminium sliding system built around standardised profiles for high-volume, cost-efficient, and reliable window and door fabrication.

The KHIDKIE™ Regular Series is built around two primary structural families: 3/4 inch profiles and 1 inch profiles, allowing fabricators and architects to select the appropriate strength, glass capacity, and frame depth based on project requirements. This dual-format architecture makes the system suitable for both lightweight residential windows and larger, heavier sliding openings.

The sliding foundation of the system is created using dedicated 2 Track, 3 Track, and 4 Track Top and Bottom profiles. These tracks define the number of moving shutters, control panel alignment, and manage load distribution. The Regular Series offers full track families in both 3/4" and 1" widths, enabling flexible panel layouts without changing the core fabrication logic.

Frame stability and locking geometry are controlled using purpose-built Handle, Interlock, Middle, and Top-Bottom profiles. These profiles ensure proper glass holding, smooth sliding motion, and correct panel engagement when the shutters meet. For higher strength requirements, the Regular Series includes a dedicated 60MM profile family that provides increased frame depth and rigidity.

Unlike improvised sliding systems that mix unrelated sections, the Regular Series is a fully structured aluminium ecosystem. Every top, bottom, handle, and interlock profile is dimensionally coordinated to ensure:
• Smooth shutter movement
• Stable panel alignment
• Predictable glass seating
• Fast fabrication and installation
• Long-term operational reliability

This engineering consistency is what makes the Regular Series the preferred choice for high-volume housing projects, commercial buildings, and everyday residential construction. It is not a designer system — it is a workhorse architecture platform that delivers dependable performance across thousands of openings, year after year.
