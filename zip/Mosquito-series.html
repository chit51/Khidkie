KHIDKIE™ SLIDING MOSQUITO NET SERIES

A simple, durable, and user-friendly mosquito protection system designed for everyday residential windows and balcony openings.

The Sliding Mosquito Net Series from Khidkie™ is a practical aluminium-based system engineered to integrate seamlessly with sliding and openable window configurations. The system is built around three essential profiles that together form a stable and smooth-operating mosquito net assembly.

The system begins with the Sliding Net Handle (SSMO 001), which provides a comfortable grip and allows effortless side-to-side movement of the net shutter. This handle profile also contributes to maintaining shutter rigidity during operation.

Structural alignment and mesh support are managed by the Sliding Net Vertical (SSMO 002). This vertical profile ensures proper tension of the mosquito mesh while keeping the shutter straight and stable during sliding.

Smooth guidance and noise-free movement are achieved using the Sliding Net Top-Bottom profile (SSMO 003), which acts as both the upper and lower guide track. This profile ensures consistent movement, prevents derailment, and supports long-term durability with minimal maintenance.

Unlike roller-based systems, the Sliding Mosquito Net operates without springs or retracting mechanisms. This makes it highly reliable for daily use, especially in residential environments where simplicity, longevity, and ease of repair are critical.

The Sliding Mosquito Net Series is best suited for bedroom windows, kitchen windows, balcony sliders, and utility areas where continuous ventilation is required along with effective insect protection. Its straightforward construction and proven logic make it a preferred choice for fabricators and homeowners alike.

KHIDKIE™ ROLLER MOSQUITO NET SERIES

A compact, retractable mosquito protection system designed for modern spaces where minimal visual presence and flexible usage are important.

The Roller Mosquito Net Series from Khidkie™ is engineered for applications where the mosquito net is required only when needed and should remain hidden when not in use. The system operates on a vertical rolling mechanism housed within a dedicated top profile.

User interaction with the system is handled through the Roller Net Handle (SRMN 001), which allows smooth pull-down and controlled retraction of the mosquito mesh. The handle is designed for easy grip and balanced movement.

Side guidance and mesh stability are maintained by the Roller Net Vertical (SRMN 002). This profile ensures that the net moves straight during operation and remains properly aligned during both extension and retraction.

The core of the system lies in the Roller Net Top (SRMN 003), which houses the rolling mechanism. This top cassette allows the mesh to retract smoothly into a compact enclosure, protecting it from dust and damage when not in use.

Compared to sliding mosquito nets, the roller system offers a cleaner visual appearance and greater flexibility in compact spaces. However, it relies on precise installation and proper handling to maintain consistent performance over time.

The Roller Mosquito Net Series is ideal for premium residences, compact window openings, doors, and locations where aesthetics and space optimisation take priority alongside insect protection.
