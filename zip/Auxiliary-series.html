About Auxiliary Aluminium Series

The Auxiliary Aluminium Series is Khidkie™’s complete support and finishing system for aluminium architecture. While primary profiles form windows, doors and partitions, it is these auxiliary profiles that ensure glass is held correctly, frames remain rigid, mosquito shutters operate smoothly and all joints, corners and edges stay aligned for years.

Where Auxiliary Series Works Best

This system is used in sliding windows, hinged doors, office partitions, mosquito shutters, glass facades, railings, furniture frames and interior installations. It supports everything from light residential work to heavy commercial construction.

Why Fabricators Depend on Auxiliary Series

Fabricators rely on these profiles because they provide the correct aluminium geometry for glass holding, frame joining, corner strengthening and finishing. This reduces site issues, improves alignment and gives a clean, professional end result.

KHIDKIE™ AUXILIARY ALUMINIUM SYSTEM

A modular aluminium support, fixing and finishing system built around precision angles, pattis, channels and clips for professional window, door, partition and mosquito installations.

The Auxiliary Aluminium Series consists of multiple functional profile families including structural angles, T-pattis, U-channels and glass-fixing clips. These profiles form the mechanical foundation that holds glass, mesh, panels and frames in position across every Khidkie™ aluminium system.

These profiles are used as secondary structural members in windows, doors, partitions, mosquito shutters, railings and architectural assemblies. They define how glass is seated, how frames are joined, how corners stay square and how panels remain stable during long-term use.

Unlike improvised site-cut aluminium, Khidkie™ Auxiliary profiles are manufactured with controlled geometry and consistent wall thickness to ensure:
• Accurate glass holding
• Strong corner reinforcement
• Rattle-free mesh and panels
• Clean finishing lines
• Long-term alignment and durability

This makes the Auxiliary Aluminium Series the invisible engineering layer behind every high-quality Khidkie™ window, door and partition system.
