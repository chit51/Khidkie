The robust, scalable, and performance-driven aluminium sliding system engineered for modern residential and architectural openings.

About Cardinal Series

Engineered as a complete sliding ecosystem, the Cardinal Series delivers precise load handling, smooth long-term performance, and clear system segmentation from light to heavy-duty applications.
Built for real project conditions, it ensures fabrication ease, structural reliability, and consistent sliding accuracy across residential, commercial, and large-span architectural openings.

Where Cardinal Works Best

Cardinal is best suited for projects that demand higher strength and flexibility — including wide residential openings, balcony windows and doors, commercial interiors, and large sliding applications where shutter size and load vary significantly.

Why Fabricators Prefer Cardinal

Fabricators prefer Cardinal for its clear system segmentation, stronger profiles, availability of multiple track, shutter and interlock options, and predictable performance across light, medium and heavy-duty sliding requirements.

KHIDKIE™ 26MM Cardinal SERIES

Compact. Precise. Efficient. Lightweight Sliding System for Standard Residential Applications

The 26MM Cardinal System is engineered for light to medium-duty sliding applications, where space efficiency, smooth movement, and cost-effectiveness are essential. With a slim 26mm profile depth, this system is ideal for standard residential windows and doors that require reliable daily operation without unnecessary bulk, offering multiple track options with and without grill integration. The system focuses on ease of fabrication, smooth sliding action, and efficient material usage.

Key Characteristics

Optimised 26mm profile depth for compact installations.
Smooth sliding performance with balanced weight distribution suitable for 2-track and 3-track window configurations Clean, minimal visual appearance.

Ideal Applications
• Residential sliding windows
• Bedrooms, kitchens, and utility areas
• Budget-optimised projects without compromising quality

✔

Grill-Integrated Track System

Dedicated 2T and 3T grill-track profiles allow secure installations without compromising sliding performance.

✔

V Track Cap Accessories

V-type and U-type track caps improve shutter guidance, finishing quality and long-term track protection.

✔

Lightweight & Fabricator-Friendly

Compact profile geometry enables faster fabrication, easy installation and reliable day-to-day operation.

KHIDKIE™ 35×75 MM Cardinal SERIES

The 35×75 Cardinal System is a true heavy-duty architectural sliding solution, purpose-built for large spans, heavy glass panels, and demanding architectural conditions. With a deep 75mm frame and reinforced 35mm profile structure, this system delivers exceptional load-bearing capacity and long-term operational stability under extreme conditions.

Key Characteristics

• Deep 35×75mm frame for maximum strength
• Designed for very heavy shutters and large glass panels
• Excellent resistance to sagging, twisting, and misalignment
• Ideal for premium and high-value architectural projects

Ideal Applications

• Commercial facades and showrooms
• Villas, hotels, and luxury residences
• High-wind, Heavy glass and high-load environments
