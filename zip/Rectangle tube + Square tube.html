About Rectangle & Square Tube Series

The Rectangle & Square Tube Series is Khidkie™’s core aluminium structural platform. Unlike decorative profiles, these hollow sections are engineered to carry loads, maintain straightness and provide long-term dimensional stability in architectural and industrial applications.

Where Tube Series Works Best

These profiles are used in gates, railings, furniture frames, glass support structures, partitions, hoardings, solar mounting systems and industrial fabrication. They are ideal wherever strength, straightness and clean welding are required.

Why Fabricators Prefer Tube Series

Fabricators prefer these tubes because of consistent wall thickness, accurate dimensions and excellent weldability. This results in stronger joints, faster fabrication and better final product quality.

KHIDKIE™ TUBE SYSTEM

A modular aluminium hollow section system built around precision rectangle and square tubes for load-bearing, framing and fabrication applications.

The Tube Series consists of multiple size families including rectangle tubes from 9×16 mm up to 125×25 mm and square tubes from 25×25 mm to 50×50 mm. This wide range allows fabricators to select the correct section based on load, span and structural requirement.

These tubes are used as primary and secondary structural members in railings, partitions, frames and architectural installations. Their hollow-section geometry provides high strength-to-weight ratio while keeping overall material usage efficient.

Unlike rolled or bent profiles, the Khidkie™ Tube Series is produced with controlled wall thickness and tight dimensional tolerances. This ensures:
• Straight and square frames
• Strong welded joints
• Uniform load distribution
• Clean visual finish
• Long-term dimensional stability

This makes the Tube Series the backbone of aluminium fabrication for architectural, commercial and industrial projects across India.
