About Z-Openable R40 & R50 Systems

The Z-Openable Series from Khidkie™ is designed for projects where you require a proper openable (casement) system instead of sliding — better sealing, better locking and a more traditional, solid feel. R40 and R50 together cover a wide range of applications: from standard room windows to large, heavy shutters and French doors.

Where Z-Openable Works Best

Z-Openable systems are ideal for rooms that need proper ventilation and tight sealing — bedrooms, kitchens, living rooms, home offices and street-facing windows. They’re also a great fit for villas, bungalows and premium apartments where clients prefer the classic “door-like” openable feel with strong hardware.

Why Fabricators Prefer R40 & R50

The R40 system is optimised for regular, everyday openable windows with smart weight control and multiple shutter options. R50, on the other hand, is a heavier system for big sizes and stronger loads. Both systems are designed to work with standard market hardware, multipoint locks, friction stays, butt hinges and DGU options — making fabrication predictable, consistent and profitable.

KHIDKIE™ R40 Z-OPENABLE SERIES

Smart, versatile openable system for modern residential windows. Perfect balance between profile weight, strength and aesthetics.

The R40 Z-Openable Series is designed as a complete ecosystem for openable windows. With multiple frame options (single, 2-in-1, 3-in-1), a rich shutter family (51mm, 65mm, 23×50, profile and molding shutters) and matching mullions, clips and DGU shutter options, R40 gives fabricators the flexibility to handle almost any standard residential requirement with one system.

Weight ranges are carefully selected to give the right balance — strong enough to feel solid and premium, but not unnecessarily heavy. Combined with the right hardware, EPDM gaskets and glass selection, R40 can deliver excellent sealing, wind resistance and user comfort for regular-sized openings.

KHIDKIE™ R50 Z-OPENABLE HEAVY DUTY SERIES

Heavy-duty Z-openable system for large, premium windows & French doors. Built for strength, stability and long-term reliability.

The R50 Z-Openable Series is the stronger, heavier brother of R40. It is engineered to handle bigger shutter sizes, heavier glass and more demanding applications like French doors, balcony doors and large openable windows. With a stronger single frame, heavier mullions and robust shutter profile, R50 is built for projects where size, performance and feel truly matter.

R50 maintains compatibility with standard high-quality hardware — heavy friction stays, multipoint locks, strong hinges and DGU clips. This allows fabricators to offer a truly premium openable solution without compromising on practicality and ease of assembly.

Key Features of Z-Openable Systems

Better Sealing

Openable casement design allows for tighter sealing compared to typical sliding windows, helping with dust, sound and wind resistance.

Wind & Weather Performance

Strong frames and mullions with proper gasket design help the system perform in high-wind and monsoon-prone regions.

Noise & Comfort

Supports single and double glazing (DGU) options for enhanced acoustic comfort in busy and noisy locations.

Secure Locking

Compatible with multipoint locks, friction stays and robust hinges for improved security and safety.

Premium Finishes

Available in anodized, powder coated, matte black and customised finishes to match interiors and exteriors.

Value & Versatility

R40 & R50 together cover small windows to heavy doors, giving fabricators a powerful yet practical system range.
